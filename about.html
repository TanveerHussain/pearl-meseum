His father's trade

A pearl merchant's son.

Sultan Ali Al-Owais was the son of Ali Bin Abdullah Al Owais, a successful pearl merchant and businessman.

His father and his tribe, the Shamsis of Sharjah, had once prospered from the pearl industry.

On the merchant's table

A reconstruction of a pearl merchant's table, photographed for this page. These are not objects from the collection.

Two worlds

Between the dive and the bank.

Even as recently as the 1950s, “the dive” was a prominent feature of life on what was then known as the Trucial coast.

Sultan pursued a career in international banking, becoming a founder of the UAE's first National Bank.

Yet he held a deep respect for the pearl industry from which his father and tribe had once prospered.

From the first pearl to 1963

  • 19 June 1963H.H. Late Sheikh Rashid bin Saeed Al Maktoum signed the Charter of Incorporation of the National Bank of Dubai, the first National Bank established in Dubai and the UAE.
  • His partA founder of the bank, and its Chairman.
  • 06 March 2007NBD merged with Emirates Bank International to form Emirates NBD.

He is regarded as one of the Nation's foremost poetic talents.

The collector

He kept them.

To preserve the memory of the Nation's traditional way of life, Sultan amassed one of the world's largest and finest collections of pearls, now on display at the Emirates NBD Pearl Museum.

“Sultan Ali Al-Owais's natural marine collection is truly one of a kind; famous not only for its immense size and value, but for the variety and quality of its contents.”
