Where to find us

Emirates NBD Head Office, Dubai Creek, Deira

Where

On the Deira bank of Dubai Creek.

The museum is inside the Emirates NBD Head Office on Baniyas Road, looking across the water to Bur Dubai, on the Creek where Dubai's pearling fleet once came home.
